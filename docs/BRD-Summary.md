# Business Requirements Summary

*Condensed from the project report; not a formally approved BRD.*

- **Business question:** How did sales change by year and where are the largest customer, regional, product, and category totals?
- **Decision supported:** Focus sales follow-up and assortment review; pair sales with margin before profitability decisions.
- **Scope:** 2021–2022 sales totals and top customer/product, region, and category views.
- **Open requirement:** Obtain the matching Excel source and confirm currency, period, and aggregation.
