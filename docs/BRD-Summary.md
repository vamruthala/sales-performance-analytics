# Business Requirements Summary

- **Business question:** I asked how sales changed by year and where the largest customer, regional, product, and category totals occurred.
- **Decision supported:** I use the analysis to focus sales follow-up and assortment review, pairing sales with margin for profitability decisions.
- **Scope:** I compare 2021–2022 sales totals and leading customers, products, regions, and categories.
- **Success measures:** I track total sales, year-over-year growth, and contribution by customer, region, product, and category.
